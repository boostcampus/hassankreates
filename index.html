FAQ
Questions

    

      
Is KreateBiz free?
Yes. Free forever plan with generous limits. Plus a 14-day Pro trial on signup and an invitation to try Business free for 7 days once you start using the app seriously.

      
What's the difference between Pro and Business?
Pro gives you unlimited tools to manage your business. Business is a different level — it lets you run your business and your people and your money and your stock and your growth, all connected.

      
Do I need a card for the trial or gift?
No. No card, no payment details, no catch. It's a real gift.

      
What happens when my subscription ends?
Your data stays. Everything you created during the trial, gift or paid plan stays accessible forever. Only the ongoing premium features lock. You drop back to the Free plan gracefully, and we'll remember you as a past customer.
