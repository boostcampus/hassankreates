Learn How to Use KreateBiz

Step-by-step guides for every free tool.

📊
How to Track Sales
Step 1: Open Sales Tracker. Step 2: Enter the product or service. Step 3: Enter quantity and price. Step 4: Save the sale. Step 5: Check your dashboard.
📉
How to Track Expenses
Open Expense Tracker, enter the expense name, category, amount and date. Save it to update your dashboard automatically.
📦
How to Manage Inventory
Add products, set stock levels, and use Stock In/Out to update inventory in real time.
🧾
How to Create an Invoice
Fill in your business and customer info, add items, and generate a professional invoice you can print or share.
📝
How to Create a Quotation
Enter your details, add the items you're quoting for, set a discount and validity date, then generate a formal quotation.
⏳
How to Track Debtors
Record amounts owed, set due dates, and send WhatsApp reminders to customers who owe you.
🧮
How to Calculate Profit
Enter your costs and desired profit percentage to get a suggested selling price instantly.
📱
How to Use the Dashboard
Your dashboard updates automatically based on your sales, expenses and stock. It shows your real business performance.
