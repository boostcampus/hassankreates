Learn How to Use KreateBiz

Step-by-step guides for every free tool.

How to Track Sales
Step 1: Open Sales Tracker. Step 2: Enter the product or service. Step 3: Enter quantity and price. Step 4: Save the sale. Step 5: Check your dashboard.
📉
How to Track Expenses
Open Expense Tracker, enter the expense name, category, amount, and date. Save it to update your dashboard automatically.
📦
How to Manage Inventory
Add products, set stock levels, and use Stock In/Out to update inventory in real time.
